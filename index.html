What it does for you

  • Builds your shop page

    Add your products and get a professional page with prices, photos, reviews and a link you can share.

  • Answers customers for you

    Replies to product and price questions in several languages, and passes hard questions to you.

  • Handles orders

    Collects orders, tracks payment, watches your stock and notifies you and your staff.

  • Plans your marketing

    Writes captions and ads, plans your month of posts and schedules the ones you approve.

  • Organizes your team and deliveries

    Staff accept orders, and customers follow their delivery with a tracking link.

  • Shows what is working

    Weekly reports on sales, best sellers and missed chances, plus ratings from happy customers.
